# Race character source

`horse-jockey.glb` is the embedded model extracted, without geometry or rig changes, from the user-provided `gait_viewer_standalone (17).html`.

The original nine animation clips are retained. Player/opponent uniform colors are applied at runtime; the source asset remains unchanged. No third-party asset license or broader reuse rights have been independently verified.

The viewer uses locally vendored Three.js 0.184.0 with its MIT license at `vendor/three/LICENSE`.
